Persistent state

Use these markers for the new Exhaustion rule and Front taxes. Keep the source card visible or nearby when a marker has a narrower target or expiry.

Exhaustion

After you lose a Front, every Force you have there receives one Exhaustion token, maximum one. It persists between Battles and normally prevents that Force from initiating a Maneuver.

EXHAUSTION

There are 24 tokens - enough for every Force position on both sides to be Exhausted simultaneously. Move the token with its Force and remove it when that Force leaves the battlefield.
