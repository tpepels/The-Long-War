The Long War — D6 kit

Hidden Battle Plans

Print and cut one four-card set per player. Each Battle, select one Plan secretly and reveal both together. The Plans are reusable and separate from the draw deck. They never change dice or Force.

Player A

Assault

Movement: Before combat: no movement.

Combat: Strikes target foremost enemy.

After casualties: advance one surviving Force one empty rank forward in each Front.

One army-wide plan — D6 paper prototype

Hold

Movement: Before combat: withdraw one Force one empty rank backward in each Front.

Combat: Strikes target foremost enemy. Protect may defend any Force in its Front.

No movement after combat.

One army-wide plan — D6 paper prototype

Volley

Movement: Before combat: no movement.

Combat: Archers can Strike any enemy rank in their Front; others target foremost.

No movement after combat.

One army-wide plan — D6 paper prototype

Raid

Movement: Before combat: move one Raider/Rider/Skirmisher per Front into a vacant same-rank adjacent active Front.

Combat: Mobile Strikes target enemy Middle/Rear if present, otherwise foremost. Others target foremost.

No movement after combat.

One army-wide plan — D6 paper prototype
