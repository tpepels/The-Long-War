The Long War — paper kit

Your Battle Plans and Command card

Each player gets four reusable, secret Battle Plans and one visible Command card. Everything about specific plans and Command is printed on the cards. The rulebook only explains the game loop.

Player A

Assault

After combat, advance one surviving Force one empty rank forward in each Front.

Press the line · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Hold

Before rolling, withdraw one Force one empty rank per Front. Your Defend results can protect any ally in their Front.

Protect your army · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Volley

Your Archers may Attack any enemy in their Front, including Middle and Rear.

Target support · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Raid

Before rolling, move one Raider, Rider or Skirmisher per Front sideways into an empty space in an adjacent open Front, staying in its rank. They may Attack enemy Middle or Rear.

Strike from the sides · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Command card

START20 Command · Draw 10 · Choose first player. Fronts 2 and 3 open; Front 1 opens in Battle II; Front 4 in Battle III.
YOUR TURNPass before drawing, or draw 1 (hand limit 10), then take up to 2 actions: Play a Force in an empty legal space for its printed cost, or Move one Force a vacant rank forward/back or sideways to the same rank in an adjacent open Front for 1 Command. Never spend below 0.
PASSOpponent takes one final turn, then reveal Plans. Non-passer starts next Battle.
ATTACK · DEFEND · SAVEAttack foremost enemy unless a card/Plan permits another. Each extra uncancelled Attack at one target grants +1 Force to all attackers. Higher Force defeats; ties survive. Defend cancels 1 Attack; Save prevents 1 whole defeat. Resolve simultaneously.
AFTER COMBATMore Forces controls a Front. Lose 1 Command per enemy-controlled Front. At 0 you lose (both 0: draw). Otherwise recover 5 Command (max 20), draw to 10. Reshuffle ordinary discards, never casualties.
FINAL BATTLEAfter Battle VIII, more controlled Fronts wins; tie goes to higher Command, then draw.
