The Long War — paper kit

Your Battle Plans and Command card

Each player gets four reusable, secret Battle Plans and one visible Command card. Everything about specific plans and Command is printed on the cards. The rulebook only explains the game loop.

Player A

Assault

After combat, advance one surviving Force one empty rank forward in each Front.

Press the line · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Hold

Before rolling, withdraw one Force one empty rank per Front. Your Defend results can protect any ally in their Front.

Protect your army · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Volley

Your Archers may Attack any enemy in their Front, including Middle and Rear.

Target support · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Raid

Before rolling, move one Raider, Rider or Skirmisher per Front sideways into an empty space in an adjacent open Front, staying in its rank. They may Attack enemy Middle or Rear.

Strike from the sides · Secret army-wide Battle Plan

Command card

START20 Command · Draw 10 cards · Random player starts. Fronts 2 + 3 open first; Front 1 in Battle II; Front 4 in Battle III.
YOUR TURNBefore drawing, you may Pass. Otherwise draw 1 (hand limit 10), then do up to 2 actions: Play a Force in an empty legal space for its printed cost; or Move one Force one vacant rank forward/back or sideways to the same rank in an adjacent open Front for 1 Command. Never spend below 0.
PASSThe opponent takes one final normal turn. Reveal Plans, then fight. The non-passer starts the next Battle.
ATTACK · DEFEND · SAVEAttack the foremost enemy unless your card or Plan permits another target. Each extra uncancelled Attack at the same enemy gives +1 Force to every attacker. Higher Force defeats; a tie survives. Defend cancels 1 Attack. Save prevents 1 Force's defeat. Resolve everything simultaneously.
AFTER COMBATMajority of surviving Forces controls each Front. Lose 1 Command for every enemy-controlled Front. At 0, lose (both 0: draw). Otherwise recover 5 Command (max 20), draw up to 10. Reshuffle normal discards if needed; defeated Forces never return.
FINAL BATTLEAfter Battle VIII, more controlled Fronts wins; tie goes to higher Command, then draw.
