The Long War · playtest

Four Fronts. One roll. One hidden Order.

A two-player tabletop war game with persistent Forces and Holdings, immediate Tactics, secret Orders and D6 combat.

Print & play

You can play the physical game now without the webgame. Open Decks, choose any two 24-card D6 playtest decks, and print them at 100% scale in A4 landscape. Then print the Short rules, Reference, and two separate 12-card Order decks. Disable browser headers and footers for card sheets.

Playtest status

The paper D6 edition is an unbalanced experimental prototype. The paper War decks now mix Forces, Holdings and Tactics. The engine, AI and historical 131-card data remain unchanged and follow an older ruleset.
