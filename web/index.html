Print & play

You can play the physical game now without the webgame. Open Decks, choose any two 48-card playtest decks, and print them at 100% scale in A4 landscape. Then print the Rulebook, Reference, and optional marker sheet. Disable browser headers and footers for card sheets.

Playtest status

The printed cards and physical rules are the current playtest package. The Webgame remains an experimental implementation and may lag behind card or rules changes while the physical game is being refined.
