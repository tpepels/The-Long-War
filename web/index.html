The Long War · playtest

Four Fronts. One roll. One hidden plan.

A two-player experimental tabletop game of persistent Forces, D6 combat, hidden army-wide Battle Plans and permanent casualties.

Print & play

You can play the physical game now without the webgame. Open Decks, choose any two 24-card D6 playtest decks, and print them at 100% scale in A4 landscape. Then print the Rulebook, Reference, and reusable Battle Plan cards. Disable browser headers and footers for card sheets.

Playtest status

The paper D6 edition is an unbalanced experimental prototype. It currently uses Forces and Force-mode Heroes only; the engine, AI and historical 131-card data remain unchanged and follow an older ruleset.
