THE LONG WAR · BATTLEFIELD OVERVIEW

Form your line. Take two Fronts.

Each Front has a Frontline and Rear. Place Subjects where their roles work best, then decide how many more cards that Front is worth.

1

Three Fronts

Left, Center, and Right score separately. Control two to win the Battle.

2

Line Defense

A Subject in the Frontline gets +1 Strength, unless a card says otherwise.

3

Front and Rear work together

Archers want a Subject in front. Spearmen want support behind. Healers strengthen the Subject directly in front.

4

Adjacent means sideways

Adjacent Subjects are immediately left or right in the same rank. Avaros rewards this formation.

5

One Hero

Every deck has exactly one Hero: a unique Subject with a special rule and an ordinary battlefield position.

6

One Stratagem per Battle

Before your normal action, you may set one Stratagem face-down. It affects the Battle as a whole and stays in play after revealing.

THE LONG WAR · FIRST-GAME REFERENCE

Turn reference

Keep this page beside the board. Check the position, take one action, and stop spending when the Front is no longer worth another card.

SETUP

Bring one Hero

30 cards · exactly 1 Hero · draw 10 · mulligan up to 2 · random first player.

YOUR TURN

Set, then act

Before your normal action, you may set your one Stratagem for this Battle. Then play 1 card or Pass.

Setting a Stratagem is free. No automatic draw. No attack phase.
WHEN PLACING A SUBJECT

Frontline: +1 Line Defense. Then apply the Subject's role.

Rear: no Line Defense. Archers, Healers, Ships, and Strongholds gain value there.

PASS

You take no more turns

Your opponent may keep playing one card per turn until they also Pass.

Passing also ends your chance to set a Stratagem this Battle.
WHEN BOTH PASS

Score in this order

  1. Who controls 2 of 3 Fronts?
  2. If neither: higher total Strength.
  3. If still tied: first passer.
BETWEEN BATTLES

Reset the board, not your hand

After scoring, reveal any still-hidden Stratagem, then discard the battlefield. Keep your hand, draw 3, and let the Battle loser choose who starts next.
