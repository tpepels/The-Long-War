Deploy

Draw 1 (hand limit 10). Take up to 2 actions: play one Force in a legal empty square for its Command cost, or move one Force to an adjacent empty rank/Front for 1 Command.

Pass instead of a turn to end deployment; your opponent takes one final turn.

Secret Battle Plans

PlanWhat happens
AssaultAfter combat, advance one surviving Force one empty rank per Front.
HoldWithdraw one Force per Front before rolling; Defend may cover any ally in that Front.
VolleyArchers Attack any enemy in their Front.
RaidMove one mobile Force per Front sideways to an empty adjacent open Front before rolling; mobile Forces Attack Middle/Rear.

Other Attacks target the nearest enemy rank. Choose only one plan for your entire army.

Resolve combat

  1. Reveal plans simultaneously before dice; make Hold/Raid movements.
  2. Each player rolls one D6 per occupied Front. Every friendly Force there uses its own outcome for that number.
  3. Declare Attacks and targets at the same time. Defend cancels one eligible Attack.
  4. A remaining Attack defeats if its Force is higher than the target's. Each additional uncancelled Attack at the same target adds +1 effective Force to those Attacks.
  5. Save prevents one Force's whole defeat. Do printed Specials; remove defeated Forces permanently. Surviving Forces stay.
  6. More surviving Forces controls a Front. Lose 1 Command per enemy-controlled Front. At 0 you lose; otherwise recover 5 (cap 20) and refill to 10 cards.

No wounds, status markers, Pressure, Pin, Tactical Read or Opportunities. Battle VIII: more controlled Fronts wins; ties use Command.

Four Fronts, three ranks each; rank does not affect scoring. Print the single-page rules for full setup and victory details. Paper D6 edition; engine and AI unchanged.
