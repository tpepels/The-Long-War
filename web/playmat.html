YOUR TURN
  1. One operation: play 1 card, Cycle 1 card, or Pass.
  2. Pay Command: cards use their printed cost; Cycle costs 1.
  3. Stratagem: setting it is your operation.
  4. After Pass: no more turns this Battle.
