YOUR TURN
  1. Start turn: draw {{TURN_DRAW_COUNT}}. At {{HAND_LIMIT}} cards, discard before each required draw. Reshuffle discard only if deck empties.
  2. One operation: play 1 card, Maneuver, or Pass. Pass normally requires each player to have completed {{PASS_MIN_OPERATIONS_BEFORE_SIGNAL}} operation(s).
  3. Maneuver: pay {{MANEUVER_COMMAND_COST}} Command; move a Named Formation one Front sideways in the same rank, or swap with a friendly formation.
  4. Pass: costs your operation and stays active for the rest of the Battle, even if you later act. When both players have Passed, the Battle ends.
WHERE CARDS GO
Force
Any position without a Force; enables prepared Force-dependent text.
Bond
Any position without a Bond; may be prepared, but contributes 0 Strength without a Force.
Name
Any position without a Name; may be prepared, but contributes 0 Strength without a Force.
Hero
Play as Force or Name; max {{HERO_PLAY_LIMIT_PER_BATTLE}} Hero card(s) from hand per Battle.
Narrative
Legend, Saga, Myth, Omen, Warning, Prophecy, or Conspiracy; face-up; max {{ONGOING_NARRATIVE_LIMIT}} Ongoing per player.
Stratagem
Place face-up in your separate Stratagem area; max 1 from hand per Battle; discard at Battle end.
CARD TEXT

Roles and classifications are labels. Their mechanics are printed on the cards; they do not carry hidden bonuses.

WHEN BOTH PLAYERS HAVE PASSED
  1. Resolve all 4 Fronts separately. Higher Strength wins; ties do nothing. There is no overall Battle winner.
  2. Discard incomplete formations before Retreat; Named Formations persist.
  3. In each lost Front: Rear Named Formation is driven off; Frontline Named Formation Retreats to Rear.
  4. Lose {{LOST_FRONT_COMMAND_PENALTY}} Command per unprotected Front lost. Then check Command Collapse: if either player is at or below {{COLLAPSE_THRESHOLD}}, lower Command loses; equal Command is a draw. If the war continues, recover the base amount, minimum {{RECOVERY_FLOOR}}; max total {{COMMAND_CAP}}.
