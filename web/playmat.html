Your turn

  1. Before drawing, Pass (no draw/actions), or draw one and take up to 2 Actions.
  2. Deploy a Force (printed Command price); Maneuver one step or friendly swap (1 Command); Cycle two cards to draw one.
  3. After first Pass, opponent takes a full turn, then passer a full turn. Reveal secret army-wide Battle Plans.

Plan reference

PlanEffect
AssaultEligible target: foremost; advance one unpinned survivor one rank after combat per Front.
HoldWithdraw one before combat; Protect any ally in its own Front.
VolleyArcher may target any enemy rank.
RaidMove one Raider/Rider/Skirmisher sideways before combat; mobile Strikes may choose Middle/Rear.

Resolve all Fronts simultaneously

  1. Reveal army-wide Plans; move for Hold/Raid. Roll one D6 per player per occupied Front; every Force there uses its own printed result.
  2. Declare eligible Strike targets simultaneously; choose freely within your Plan's target ranks. A solo-ineffective Strike may become Pressure instead of damage.
  3. Resolve Evade → Pressure → Protect. Pressure suppresses the target's Protect/Rescue or Pins it. Protect cancels one damaging Strike; Evade cancels all at self.
  4. Coordinate: each uncancelled damaging Strike gains +1 effective Force per other Strike at its target. Greater Force defeats; equality survives unless the Special permits equality. Failed uncancelled Strikes Pin (no after-combat move).
  5. Rescue saves one Force's entire defeat. Remove unrescued casualties permanently; perform printed movement then Assault advances. Pinned Forces cannot move.
  6. Count survivors per Front: more Forces controls it, equal counts are contested. Cross-Front effects require explicit printed Special.

Command after scoring

Lose 1 Command per enemy-controlled Front; check Collapse (0). Recover 8 if behind in controlled Fronts, 6 if ahead, 7 each if tied (cap 20). Draw up to ten; casualties never return.

Prototype restrictions: No Bonds, Names, Tactics, Orders, Stratagems, Narratives, legacy attacks, health, condition markers or old Opening Orders. Four reusable Battle Plans per player. Full rules: rulebook.html. TLW experimental paper D6
