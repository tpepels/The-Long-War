THE LONG WAR · BATTLEFIELD OVERVIEW

Form the line. Win two Fronts.

Frontline and Rear now have different jobs. Subjects gain power from their role, from who stands in front or behind them, and from who stands adjacent.

1

Three Fronts

Left, Center, and Right are separate contests. You normally need two.

2

Line Defense

Every occupied Frontline gets +1 Strength. The front card therefore counts more than the same card in the Rear.

3

Front and Rear cooperate

*Archers* want a Subject in front. *Spearmen* want support behind. *Healers* strengthen the Subject directly in front.

4

Adjacent means sideways

Adjacent Subjects are immediately left or right in the same rank. The Hero *Avaros* rewards this formation.

5

One Hero

Every deck has exactly one Hero. Heroes are stronger Subjects with special rules, but still occupy a normal position.

6

Stories change the formation

*Myths, Legends,* and *Sagas* resolve immediately. *Veiled Omens, Warnings, Prophecies,* and *Conspiracies* wait face-down.

THE LONG WAR · FIRST-GAME REFERENCE

What do I do now?

Keep this page beside the board. Read the formation first, then decide whether another card is worth spending.

SETUP

Bring one Hero

30 cards · exactly 1 Hero · draw 10 · mulligan up to 2 · random first player.

YOUR TURN

Make one commitment

Play 1 card or Pass. Then the opponent acts.

No automatic draw. No attack phase.
WHEN PLACING A SUBJECT

Frontline: +1 Line Defense. Then apply the Subject's *role*.

Rear: no Line Defense, but *Archers, Healers, Ships,* and *Strongholds* prefer it.

PASS

You are done with this Battle

Your opponent may keep playing until they also Pass.

Passing saves cards but gives up your chance to improve the formation.
WHEN BOTH PASS

Score in this order

  1. Who controls 2 of 3 Fronts?
  2. If neither: higher total Strength.
  3. If still tied: first passer.
BETWEEN BATTLES

Reset the board, not your hand

Discard the battlefield. Keep your hand. Draw 3. Battle loser chooses who starts next.
