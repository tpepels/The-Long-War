First-game reference

SETUP 30-card deck → draw 10 → mulligan up to 2 → random first player
1

Your turn

Play 1 card from your hand or Pass.

No automatic draw. No attack phase.

2

Build

Subject takes a position. Link attaches to it. Name completes the Legend.

Plot resolves now. Scheme waits face-down for its trigger.

3

Pass

Once you Pass, you are done for this Battle. The other player may keep playing until they Pass too.

4

Score the Battle

  1. Control 2 of 3 Fronts.
  2. If neither does: higher total Strength.
  3. If still tied: first passer wins.
5

Reset

Discard the battlefield. Keep your hand. Draw 3 each. Battle loser chooses who starts next.
