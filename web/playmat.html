PLAYING A CARD

What can go where?

◆ Subject
Any legal empty Frontline or Rear position.
⛓ Bond
Attach to one of your Subjects that has no Bond.
✦ Name
Attach to one of your open Bonds.
⌁ Story
Resolve its targets immediately, then discard it.
◐ Veiled Story
Face-down in a Front where you have no Veiled Story.
⚑ Stratagem
Before your normal action; one set opportunity per Battle.

SCORING

When both players Pass

  1. Front control. Higher Strength controls each Front.
  2. Battle. Control 2 Fronts to win.
  3. No one has 2? Higher total Strength wins.
  4. Still tied? The first player who Passed wins.
1Optional: set your Stratagem if unused.
2Normal action: play 1 card or Pass.
3After Pass: you take no more turns this Battle.
↻After scoring: clear battlefield, keep hand, draw 3; loser chooses next starter.
