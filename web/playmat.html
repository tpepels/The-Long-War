YOUR TURN
  1. Before drawing: choose to Pass (no draw, no Actions), or take a normal turn.
  2. Normal turn: draw {{TURN_DRAW_COUNT}}. Discard down to {{HAND_LIMIT}} if needed; reshuffle discard if the deck empties.
  3. Act: take up to {{ACTIONS_PER_TURN}} Actions: play a card, use an ACTION ability, Maneuver, Attack, or Cycle (discard 2, draw 1). Ending early is not Pass.
  4. Maneuver: pay {{MANEUVER_COMMAND_COST}} Command; move any eligible formation one adjacent active position - left/right or one rank forward/back, never diagonal - or swap with a friendly formation. Exhausted Forces have −1 Strength and normally cannot Maneuver.
  5. Pass: voluntary, before drawing. Opponent takes one full closing turn (draw + up to 2 Actions), then the passer takes one; resolve the Battle.
WHERE CARDS GO
Force
Any position without a Force, subject to printed row restrictions.
Bond
Any position without a Bond; may be prepared, but contributes 0 Strength without a Force.
Name
Any position without a Name; may be prepared. PLAY works immediately; other abilities wait for attachment to a Force.
Hero
Play at most {{HERO_FORCE_PLAY_LIMIT_PER_BATTLE}} Hero as Force and {{HERO_NAME_PLAY_LIMIT_PER_BATTLE}} Hero as Name from hand per Battle.
Narrative
Face-up until this Battle ends; maximum 4 per player. CONTINUOUS text stays active; ACTION abilities require an Action.
Tactic
One-shot hostile card; selecting a Force also targets its formation. A selected target determines its Front for Taxes.
Order
One-shot friendly card; its selected target or Front also determines any applicable Tax. Discard after resolving.
Stratagem
Choose an active Front publicly; place face-down beside it. Reveal is optional when triggered. Max {{STRATAGEM_PLAY_LIMIT_PER_BATTLE}} per player per Battle, even when set by an effect.
CARD TEXT

Stack: Force + Bond + Name; modifiers add to Force Strength, minimum 0 per formation. Attached Name classifications combine with the Force, including basic Attacks (one Attack per Force per Battle). Compatible prepared layers attach when a formation moves into them. Exhausted: −1 Strength. Depleted: −1 Strength and cannot Attack or use ACTION on any layer. Shaken: −2 Strength. Penalties stack. Flanked Frontline: −1 Strength while flanked, no marker. PLAY resolves on play (also when prepared, never replayed on attachment); BECOMES NAMED triggers on genuine completion. Here means the card's Front. Only actual harmful Force markers can be removed by marker-removal effects; bare suppression is not a marker. MOVE and SWAP are not Maneuvers.

AFTER THE TWO CLOSING TURNS
  1. Each player secretly sets 2 Opening Orders (Maneuver, Commit, Strike or Hold). Reveal together, then resolve Commit costs, numbered Maneuvers and numbered Strikes.
  2. One simultaneous resolution Stratagem reveal window, using pre-reveal board and Strength for eligibility. Prevention wins; conflicting moves cancel.
  3. Compare Strength in each active Front (minimum 0 per formation); record lost Fronts and ties. Resolve Battle-end card effects.
  4. Lose Command equal to the full Strength deficit in each lost Front; check Collapse at {{COLLAPSE_THRESHOLD}} before recovering.
  5. The loser chooses one Force per lost Front for next-Battle Exhaustion. Its Guarded may prevent it (do not choose another Force). Clear old conditions and unspent Tax markers, then apply new Exhaustion; recover, refill and open the next Front.
