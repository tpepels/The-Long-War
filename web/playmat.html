YOUR TURN
  1. Start turn: draw {{TURN_DRAW_COUNT}}. At {{HAND_LIMIT}} cards, discard before each required draw. Reshuffle discard only if deck empties.
  2. Act: take up to {{ACTIONS_PER_TURN}} Actions - play a card, Maneuver, or Cycle (discard 2, draw 1). You may repeat an Action type or end your turn early.
  3. Maneuver: pay {{MANEUVER_COMMAND_COST}} Command; move a Named Formation one active Front sideways in the same rank, or swap with a friendly formation.
  4. Pass: only when no legal Action remains after your draw. Opponent takes one full closing turn, then you take one full closing turn, then the Battle ends.

CARD TEXT

Roles and classifications are labels. Their mechanics are printed on the cards; they do not carry hidden bonuses.

AFTER THE TWO CLOSING TURNS
  1. Resolve each active Front separately. Higher Strength wins; ties cost neither player Command.
  2. Resolve Battle-end effects, then clear effects that last only for this Battle.
  3. Lose {{LOST_FRONT_COMMAND_PENALTY}} Command per unprotected Front lost, applying the loss in full even below 0. The battlefield itself does not move or clear.
  4. Check Command Collapse before recovery: lower Command loses; if both are equally at/below {{COLLAPSE_THRESHOLD}}, the player who Passed loses. If the war continues, recover and the player who did not Pass starts the next Battle.
