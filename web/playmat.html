YOUR TURN
  1. Start turn: draw 1. At 10 cards, discard 1 first.
  2. One operation: play 1 card, Maneuver, or Pass. Normally Pass becomes legal after both players have acted.
  3. Maneuver: pay 1 Command; Named Formation moves one Front sideways or swaps with a friendly formation in the same rank.
  4. First Pass: opponent draws normally, then takes one final operation.

AFTER THE FINAL OPERATION
  1. Resolve all 4 Fronts separately. Higher Strength wins; a tie does nothing. There is no overall Battle winner.
  2. Clear incomplete formations.
  3. In each lost Front: Rear Named Formation is driven off; Frontline Named Formation Retreats to Rear.
  4. Recover Command: base recovery − Fronts lost, minimum 0.
