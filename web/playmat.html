Quick reference

Turn

Play exactly one card or Pass. No automatic turn draw.

Legend

Subject → Link → Name. An open Link is normally inactive.

Battle

When both Pass, control 2 of 3 Fronts. Otherwise compare total Strength; if still tied, first passer wins.

Between Battles

Discard the battlefield. Keep hands. Draw 3 each. The loser chooses who starts next.

Schemes

Play face-down in a Front. One per player per Front. Reveal and resolve when the printed trigger occurs.

Match

First to 2 Battle wins.
