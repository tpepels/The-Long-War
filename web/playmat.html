Your turn

  1. Before drawing, Pass (no draw/actions), or draw one and take up to 2 Actions.
  2. Deploy a Force (printed Command price); Maneuver one step or friendly swap (1 Command); Cycle two cards to draw one.
  3. After first Pass, opponent takes a full turn, then passer a full turn. Reveal secret army-wide Battle Plans.

Plan reference

Strong counters: Hold > Assault > Volley > Raid > Hold. A countering Plan earns one Tactical Read per occupied Front after dice: one Strike targets any rank and bypasses 1 Protect, or one Protect cancels 1 extra Strike against an eligible friend. Neutral: Assault/Raid and Hold/Volley; mirrors get no Read.

PlanEffect
AssaultTarget foremost; one post-combat forward step per Front without Opportunity.
HoldWithdraw one; Protect any ally in its Front; advance after Opportunity.
VolleyMiddle/Rear Archer may target any enemy rank; advance after Opportunity.
RaidMobile sideways before combat; Frontline/Middle mobile attacks Middle/Rear; advance after Opportunity.

Role and rank: a Frontline Guard rolling Protect can protect any ally in its Front; a Rear Healer/Steward can Rescue any ally there. Rank never adds control points.

Resolve all Fronts simultaneously

  1. Reveal Plans and move for Hold/Raid. Roll one D6 per player per occupied Front; all friendly Forces there use their own printed face.
  2. Declare legal Strikes and Pressure simultaneously. Targets depend on Plan and role/rank. The countering Plan may nominate one Read Strike or Read Protect per Front.
  3. Resolve Evade → Pressure → Protect. Read Strike bypasses one Protect; Read Protect cancels one extra damaging Strike for an eligible target. Pressure suppresses Protect/Rescue or Pins.
  4. Each uncancelled damaging Strike gets +1 effective Force per other Strike at the same target. Greater Force defeats; equality survives unless printed Special says otherwise. Failed attacks Pin.
  5. Rescue saves one Force's entire defeat. Discard other casualties. Opportunity if your Strike discarded an enemy, Protect/Evade cancelled damage, or Rescue saved a Force.
  6. Printed advance first; then one optional forward step with Opportunity (Assault needs none). One post-combat move per player per Front; Pinned or already moved Forces cannot move. Majority controls.

Command after scoring

Lose 1 Command per enemy-controlled Front; check Collapse (0). Recover 8 if behind in controlled Fronts, 6 if ahead, 7 each if tied (cap 20). Draw up to ten; casualties never return.

Prototype restrictions: No Bonds, Names, Tactics, Orders, Stratagems, Narratives, legacy attacks, health, condition markers or old Opening Orders. Four reusable Battle Plans per player. Full rules: rulebook.html. TLW experimental paper D6
