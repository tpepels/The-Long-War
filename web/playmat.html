YOUR TURN
  1. Start turn: draw 1. At 10 cards, discard 1 first.
  2. One operation: play 1 card, Maneuver, or Pass. Pass normally requires both players to have acted.
  3. Maneuver: pay 1 Command; move a Named Formation one Front sideways in the same rank, or swap with a friendly formation.
  4. Pass: opponent takes a normal turn. If they also Pass, the Battle ends; if they act, your Pass is cleared.

AFTER TWO CONSECUTIVE PASSES
  1. Resolve all 4 Fronts separately. Higher Strength wins; ties do nothing. There is no overall Battle winner.
  2. Discard incomplete formations before Retreat; Named Formations persist.
  3. In each lost Front: Rear Named Formation is driven off; Frontline Named Formation Retreats to Rear.
  4. Recover Command: base recovery − Fronts lost, minimum 0; max total 20.
COMMAND20–1 on a d20; set it aside at 0 · Maneuver costs 1. After recovery, if either player is below 5: lower Command loses; equal means fight on.
RECOVERYBase after Battles I–VIII+: +10, +7, +5, +4, +3, +2, +1, +0… then −1 per Front lost, minimum 0.
