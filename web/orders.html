The Long War

Paper edition / tactical Orders

Choose your Order. Change the battle.

Each player has a separate 12-card Order deck. Draw three Orders, secretly choose one for the next Battle, and replenish your hand afterward. Deploying Forces, moving and passing are normal actions - there is no Command card.

1. Draw threeAt setup, each player takes three Orders from their own 12-card deck.
2. Commit oneBefore each Battle, secretly select an Order from your hand. Reveal both before rolling.
3. ReplenishDiscard the played Order after combat. Draw one new Order; keep the others.

Four identities, twelve cards

The four Order families

Each player has three copies of Assault, Hold, Volley and Raid. Your Orders are hidden until revealed, and the discarded Order cannot be selected again unless it is drawn again.

Print two separate 12-card Order decks for two players. Use A4 landscape at 100%, with background graphics enabled, headers/footers off, and identical opaque sleeves so secret Orders cannot be identified from their backs. The separate 24-card Force decks remain unchanged.
