The Long War

Paper edition / experimental design

Orders you draw, not rules you consult.

The Command card disappears: deploying Forces, moving and passing are ordinary player decisions. A separate Order deck gives each player a changing hand of hidden tactical choices. This is a proposed variant, not yet the published rules.

1. Draw threeAt setup, each player takes three Orders from their own 12-card deck.
2. Commit oneBefore each Battle, secretly select an Order from your hand. Reveal both before rolling.
3. ReplenishDiscard the played Order after combat. Draw one new Order; keep the others.

Four identities, twelve cards

The four Order families

For this first test, each player has three copies of each Order. Effects are unchanged from the current Battle Plans. They are now deliberately scarce, drawn choices rather than a permanent four-option menu.

Prototype design only. The Force decks, one-page rulebook, executable game and current Battle Plan cards are unchanged. Print on A4 landscape at 100%, using identical opaque sleeves so the face of a secret Order cannot be identified.
