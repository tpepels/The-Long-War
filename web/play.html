Three Fronts. Two Victories.

Win a Battle by controlling at least two Fronts. Win two Battles to win the match.

Your turn

Draw 1 card automatically at the start of your turn, then spend Command to play one card. A Stratagem is played face-up and uses your operation. Once both players have acted, you may Pass; the opponent then receives exactly one final operation before scoring.

Formations

A Subject, Bond and Name share one position. You can prepare a Bond or Name before its Subject. Frontline Subjects receive +1 Line Defense.

Between Battles

Keep your hand, draw pile, and discard pile. Gain 10 Command, up to 20, then refill to 10 cards. Shuffle the discard only when an empty draw pile needs to supply a card.
