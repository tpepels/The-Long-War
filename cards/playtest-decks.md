# Four combo-driven physical playtest decks

These **48-card physical-print decks** replace the old movement/scouting/control/defence archetypes. Each is built around combinations that create lasting **Strength**, protect formations, disrupt an opponent's Strength, or convert afflictions into Command and lost attachments. Movement is included only where it helps put a combo in place.

All four decks use the 131-card physical printable overlay (`cards/print-overrides.json`) and the physical rulebook. The executable Webgame card set remains separate. These are candidate playtest lists, **not proven balanced decks**.

The intended test is straightforward: play a deck as a complete plan, then compare how reliably its best two- and three-card sequences change a Front result, rather than measuring how often a movement ability activates.

## Banner & Blood

**Game plan.** Complete commanding formations, then turn Kings, Captains and Named units into permanent Strength backed by a few decisive hidden bonuses.

**Playtest question.** Can clustered leadership and completed formations win Fronts by 1–3 Strength while staying resilient when individual Names or Bonds are removed?

**Composition:** 16 Forces · 8 Bonds · 6 Names · 3 Heros · 4 Tactics · 4 Stratagems · 4 Narratives · 3 Orders. **Total:** 48 cards. Costs may differ from the base card data because print overrides apply.

### Signature combinations

- Late Banner + Marched Beneath the Banner of + Namar or Arel turns a cheaper Name into a Named leadership formation with an additional Bond Strength bonus; Namar restores Command for the next attachment.
- Avaros, the Bronze King + The Center Must Hold or Every Banner Turned Toward Them creates a concentrated final Strength swing; the Banner Stratagem can also reinforce an adjacent active Front.
- Doros, the Last Spear or A Hundred Shields + Followed + a Name forms a large persistent stack; The Battle Had Chosen Them adds a further +1 to Named formations.
- First Spear protects the Force behind it with Guarded, while Red Shields, Serekh and Alda deter hostile Tactics. The King Had Given the Order exchanges attached Bonds and gives affected Named formations +1 Strength this Battle. Re-form the Line opens overcrowded positions.

## Crown of Crows

**Game plan.** Hide durable Archer formations behind Guards, stack their Strength with completed Names, and punish attackers with delayed Archer responses.

**Playtest question.** Can a guarded Archer line convert its setup into a decisive Strength advantage without relying on surveillance or repeated relocation?

**Composition:** 16 Forces · 8 Bonds · 6 Names · 3 Heros · 4 Tactics · 4 Stratagems · 4 Narratives · 3 Orders. **Total:** 48 cards. Costs may differ from the base card data because print overrides apply.

### Signature combinations

- Crow Archers + a Bond + Corin of the High Wall or Serai, Queen of Crows combines Empowered pressure, Archer classifications, and multiple +1 Strength effects; Every Bow Was Strung rewards bonded Archers.
- Old Guard in Middle protects a Rear Archer from basic Archer Attacks. Thornbow Hunters in Rear give every other friendly Archer in their Front +1 Strength; First Spear can Guard the Middle screen.
- The Archers Were Ready lets an unspent Archer fire as a reaction to an opposing Attack; The Crows Came Down Exhausts on PLAY and can Shake a target later for a -2 Strength swing.
- Serai + other Archers + The Battle Had Chosen Them pushes Strength, while Asha, Alda, Veyra, No Step Back and Guarded protect crucial formations from hostile effects.

## Oathforge

**Game plan.** Build high-Strength Named Formations through Bond discounts, prepared layers and completion rewards, then protect the finished stacks.

**Playtest question.** Do the attachment chains create enough persistent Strength and defensive redundancy to justify setup Actions against ordinary Force deployment?

**Composition:** 16 Forces · 9 Bonds · 7 Names · 3 Heros · 4 Tactics · 3 Stratagems · 4 Narratives · 2 Orders. **Total:** 48 cards. Costs may differ from the base card data because print overrides apply.

### Signature combinations

- Late Banner + Followed + a Name yields a cheaper Named stack with an extra Bond Strength bonus; The Battle Had Chosen Them rewards that same Named state.
- Tovan, the Quartermaster discounts Bonds and Names. Torren supplies immediate Bond attachment, while Oren discounts a Bond by 1 Command once per Battle and draws if it completes a Named formation. Namar restores Command and discounts another attachment.
- Field Train and House of Reed attach prepared layers into a supported formation; Nara, Builder of Walls and Stayed Behind For complete otherwise stranded stacks.
- Teren turns Named completion into a free Stratagem setting Action; There Was No Road Back can then punish an enemy Bond or reposition a completed stack. Avaros and the Banner Stratagem convert prepared strength into a winning Front.

## Blood & Spoils

**Game plan.** Combine persistent high-Strength Raiders with Archer Exhaustion, Skirmisher Shaken penalties, Command transfers and attachment raids.

**Playtest question.** Can Exhaustion and Shaken turn into genuine Strength and Command advantages without leaving the deck unable to defend or finish a Front?

**Composition:** 17 Forces · 8 Bonds · 6 Names · 3 Heros · 4 Tactics · 4 Stratagems · 3 Narratives · 3 Orders. **Total:** 48 cards. Costs may differ from the base card data because print overrides apply.

### Signature combinations

- Crow Archers or The Baggage Was Abandoned Exhaust an enemy; The Unnamed Host + Shared the Spoils With can then transfer Command, while Brannoc or Black Company strips an attached component from the exhausted formation.
- Red Duelists and Tala apply Shaken or -2 Strength at a contested Front; Iron Boars punish an exposed support rank with Depleted, enabling The Stores Were Taken to threaten a Bond.
- Raider or Skirmisher affliction can activate The Trap Closed, returning an attached Bond/Name; All Reserves Forward and Seized the Standard of offer additional layer removal instead of relying on movement.
- Rovan, Doros and Red Shields provide durable raw Strength, while Serai + Crow Archers + The Archers Were Ready gives an independent defensive counterattack and Strength line.

## What to measure

- Count how often a signature sequence is available and whether it changes the outcome of a Front, rather than simply adding temporary markers.
- Record total Strength committed, surviving Strength, Command expenditure and whether protection forces the opponent to spend additional Actions.
- Record stranded cards and dependencies: two-card sequences should be usable without drawing the entire deck.
- Play mirrored matchups with different first players. Focus first on **Banner & Blood vs Crown of Crows**, **Crown of Crows vs Blood & Spoils**, and **Blood & Spoils vs Oathforge**; then cross-check the remaining pairings.
- Use `python tools/physical_playtest_metrics.py` with real physical-game observations. Existing Webgame simulations do **not** implement this printable overlay.
