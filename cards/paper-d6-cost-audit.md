# Paper D6 — Force deployment costs (44-card pass)

Costs are **Command spent when deploying a Force**, not a second point system or a new rule. Move still costs 1 Command; starting Command remains 20, and recovery remains 5 after each Battle, capped at 20.

## Bands

| Command | Identity | Count |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 2 | Fragile or narrow setup / protection pieces | 7 |
| 3 | Core deployment: dependable infantry and tactical support | 14 |
| 4 | Flexible, reliable, mobile or high-reach Forces | 18 |
| 5 | Exceptional Force-5/6 elites and anchors | 5 |

This is a **deliberately hand-curated initial price pass**, not an exact additive formula. Assess raw Force, attack-face reliability, target reach, defence breadth, card/Command advantage, mobility, and how conditional the Special really is. A cheap specialist can be powerful when its partners are present; a 5-cost elite should be a meaningful tempo sacrifice.

## Starter deck comparison

| Deck | Total printed deployment Command (24 cards) | Average per Force |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Shield & Volley | 84 | 3.50 |
| Riders & Raids | 81 | 3.38 |
| Sustain & Control | 77 | 3.21 |

These totals are **deck composition statistics**, not Command available at one time; only a fraction of any deck is deployed per Battle. At 20 starting Command a player can afford, for example, five 4-cost deployments or four 5-cost deployments **only if they take enough deployment actions and no other Command costs intervene**. Command attrition, rank availability and draw order will substantially change real play.

The normal D6 results are also streamlined: a useful support result, a short core attack or save, one unique signature result with its conditional effect, and basic defence. The six numerical die assignments have not changed, and all 44 cards still avoid empty outcomes.

## Required balance testing

Compare first-Battle deploy counts, Command starvation, conditional ability activation, multi-Front control and Battle VIII outcomes across the three starter decks. Include mirrors and swapped first-player/order decisions. **Passing print and structural checks cannot prove these costs are balanced.**
