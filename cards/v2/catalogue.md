# The Long War - 95-card V2 Mechanical Redesign

This catalogue accompanies `cards-v2-redesign.json`. Costs and Strength values are first-pass balance targets; the mechanical identities are the primary redesign.


## How to read these cards

This catalogue describes mechanics; the V2 card face is designed around stacking. Force Strength and Bond/Name modifiers sit in a **10 mm exposed top strip**, together with classifications and any recurring icon/reminder. The Name remains on top and carries the readable recurring rules. Command cost sits at the bottom. PLAY/NAMED text on buried cards may disappear after it has resolved; recurring or continuous information may not.

Timing vocabulary: **PLAY, NAMED, ACTION, TRIGGER, RESOLUTION, CONTINUOUS**, with a **1/BATTLE** marker where needed. Temporary effects use physical markers rather than memory.
