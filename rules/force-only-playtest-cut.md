# The Long War — Force-only tabletop cut (first post-playtest experiment)

**Status:** provisional physical-playtest rule cut, 11 October 2026. It deliberately does **not** rewrite combat, the cards, Command costs or the digital engine. The canonical rulebook, print files, art and playtest decks remain unchanged on `main`.

## What to remove

1. Take **all 24 Bonds and 20 Names** out of both decks and the game. Do not play, draw, prepare, attach, transfer, return or exchange those card types.
2. **Heroes may be played as Forces only.** Use their printed Force Strength, Force cost, Force abilities and normal Force legality. Ignore their Name side. The existing one-Hero-as-Force-per-Battle allowance still applies.
3. Each battlefield position holds **one Force or Force-mode Hero**; there are no layers, named/bonded states, prepared cards, name-granted classifications, formation-completion triggers or related stack-removal rules. A "formation" now means that single Force/Hero.
4. All other mechanics remain **unchanged for this comparison**: Front opening, three ranks, Command and Strength resolution, Actions, Maneuver, basic Attacks, flanking, conditions/Boons, Stratagems, Narratives, Opening Orders, passing and Battle persistence.
5. When an otherwise legal card has **an independent effect plus a clause involving Bond/Name/attached/prepared/Named/Bonded**, apply only the independent effect. No clause may give credit for nonexistent attachments or named states. If an entire effect requires those states, it cannot activate. A Force/Hero with no usable printed effect remains a legal Strength-only Force/Hero.
6. Set aside any non-Force card whose **whole effect** requires the removed mechanics (list below). Do not replace it with a redesigned ability for this test.
7. Do not treat the current published 48-card decks as ready to play after simply pulling cards out; they become undersized. Build equal legal decks from retained cards before comparing outcomes. No card additions or cost/Strength rebalancing are authorized by this cut.

Of the **87** identities remaining immediately after deleting Bond and Name cards, **27** still have direct printed references to the removed mechanics. **60** identities have no such direct references. Of the 27, 12 are Forces or Force-mode Heroes whose obsolete effect is ignored, 5 are other cards that retain an independent portion of an effect, and 10 are unplayable without redesign.

### Ten additional non-Force cards to set aside (no surviving standalone effect)

- **Tactics:** They Returned With Names; The Muster Was False; The Line Wheeled; All Reserves Forward; The Stores Were Taken.
- **Stratagems:** The Ground Was Held; The Trap Closed; There Was No Road Back.
- **Narratives:** The King Had Given the Order; Every Bow Was Strung.

### Five partially usable non-Force cards

- **All Banners Forward:** keep the King/Captain-targeted Shaken effect; ignore the Named-dependent second penalty.
- **They Let Them Through:** keep the targeted formation's ACTION lock; ignore the Name Strength clause.
- **No Road Was Too Long:** keep the legal two-Front exchange; ignore prepared layers and the Named Strength bonus.
- **The Battle Had Chosen Them:** apply +1 Strength to friendly Hero Forces only (not any Named formations).
- **Re-form the Line:** keep the Captain-enabled adjacent swap; in the other alternative, discard the chosen friendly Force without attachments to regain 2 Command.

### Twelve Forces / Force-mode Heroes whose printed effect becomes unavailable

Seven Black Ships; The House of Reed; The Dust Riders; The Black Pursuers; The Late Banner; The Banner Singers; The Field Train; The River Raiders; Rovan, the Gatebreaker; Nara, Builder of Walls; Serai, Queen of Crows; Doros, the Last Spear.

Their bodies and costs are retained *as printed*. No compensating ability is invented.

## What this experiment does **not** test

This cut removes formation-stack bookkeeping, but the game still has four basic Attack types, several status markers and Boons, hidden Stratagem timing, Narratives, three ranks, flanking and two secret Opening Orders each Battle. It also keeps **Command loss equal to the Strength margin**, which may continue to make raw Strength dominant. Those are separate potential problems; do not change them simultaneously if the aim is to see exactly what removing Bond/Name contributes.

**Verification status:** this is a static inventory/rules cut, not a played or simulated result. Existing decks, print layout, rulebook and engine have **not** been regenerated for this experiment. Do not call the present published kit a validated Force-only edition.
