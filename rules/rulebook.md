# The Long War

*Build an army, choose secret Orders and control the battlefield. Survivors stay; casualties never return.*

## Prepare

Each player takes a **24-card Force deck**, a separate **12-card Order deck**, **20 Command** and a D6. Shuffle both decks. Draw **10 Forces** and **3 Orders**. Fronts 2 and 3 open first; Front 1 opens in Battle II and Front 4 in Battle III.

## Deploy

Take turns: draw one Force (hand limit 10), then take **up to two actions**. Play a Force in an empty permitted position for its Command cost, or move a Force one rank forward/back or sideways to the next open Front in the same rank for **1 Command**. The destination must be empty. Never spend below zero. You may **Pass** instead of taking a turn; your opponent gets one final turn.

## Order

Each player secretly commits **one Order from their hand**. Reveal both before rolling. Follow the Order cards.

## Fight

Roll **one D6 per occupied Front**. Every friendly Force there uses its own result for that roll. Declare targets together; resolve effects simultaneously. Attack the foremost enemy unless a card says otherwise. An uncancelled Attack beats lower Force; each extra uncancelled Attack on the same target gives **+1 Force to every attacker**. Defend cancels one Attack; Save prevents one defeat.

## Continue

More surviving Forces controls a Front. Lose **1 Command per enemy-controlled Front**; at zero, lose (both zero: draw). Otherwise recover **5 Command** (maximum 20), refill to 10 Forces (reshuffle ordinary discards, not casualties, if needed), discard your played Order and draw one replacement. The non-passer starts the next Battle. After Battle VIII, more controlled Fronts wins; ties go to more Command, then draw.
