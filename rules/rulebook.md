# The Long War

*Four Fronts. One hand. One long war.*

<div class="rulebook-opening" markdown="1">

**The Long War** is a two-player card game of persistent formations and attrition. You fight across four Fronts, spend Command to shape the position, and decide when the current Battle is no longer worth the cost.

A complete **Force - Bond - Name** is a **Named Formation**. Named Formations can survive from one Battle to the next. Lose their Front and they are pushed back: Frontline formations Retreat to the Rear; Rear formations are driven off.

Battles are not scored as wins or losses. Their consequences remain on the table. The war ends through **Command Collapse**.

</div>

## Goal and battlefield {#learn}

The battlefield has **four Fronts**. Each Front has two positions on each player's side:

- **Frontline**
- **Rear**

                         OPPONENT

              FRONT 1   FRONT 2   FRONT 3   FRONT 4
                Rear      Rear      Rear      Rear
             Frontline Frontline Frontline Frontline
        =================================================
                       BATTLE LINE
        =================================================
             Frontline Frontline Frontline Frontline
                Rear      Rear      Rear      Rear
              FRONT 1   FRONT 2   FRONT 3   FRONT 4

                           YOU

At the end of a Battle, compare the total Strength on each Front.

- Higher Strength wins that Front.
- Equal Strength is a tie. Nothing happens there.
- There is **no overall Battle winner**.

Losing a Front can drive formations backward or off the battlefield and reduces the Command you recover.

## Setup {#setup}

1. Each player takes a **34-card playtest deck**.
2. Set your Command to **20** on a d20.
3. Shuffle and draw **10 cards**.
4. You may shuffle up to 2 cards from your hand back into your deck, then draw the same number.
5. Randomly choose the first player.

Hands and decks are hidden. Battlefield cards, discard piles, Stories, Stratagems, hand size, deck size, and Command are public.

The first player's first turn begins normally. Because the opening hand already contains 10 cards, that player discards 1 card before making the normal draw.

<div class="rulebook-kicker">PLAYING A BATTLE</div>

## Your turn {#turn}

At the start of every turn, **draw 1 card**.

Your maximum hand size is **10**. Whenever you would draw while holding 10 cards:

1. discard 1 card from your hand;
2. then draw.

After drawing, take exactly **one operation**:

1. **Play one card** and pay its printed Command cost.
2. **Maneuver** one Named Formation for 1 Command.
3. **Pass** for 0 Command, when Pass is legal.

You cannot spend more Command than you have. Command never goes below 0.

Cost reductions cannot reduce a printed card below 1 Command unless a card explicitly says otherwise.

There is no generic Draw operation, no generic Advance, and no attack step.

## Force - Bond - Name {#cards}

### Vocabulary

- **Force**, **Bond**, and **Name** refer to the card types themselves.
- A **formation** is a Force together with any Bond and/or Name in the same position.
- A formation **has a Bond** if a Bond is present, whether or not it has a Name.
- A formation **has a Name** if a Name is present, whether or not it has a Bond.
- A **Named Formation** - or a formation that **is Named** - contains all three: **Force + Bond + Name**.
- A **prepared Bond** or **prepared Name** is in a battlefield position with no Force yet. It is not a formation.
- An **open Bond** is a Bond attached to a Force when that formation has no Name.
- The word **Named** is reserved for a complete Force + Bond + Name. A Force + Name without a Bond is not Named.

Each battlefield position can hold at most one **Force**, one **Bond**, and one **Name**.

The three components may be played in **any order**.

### Force

A Force activates the formation and contributes its printed Strength.

Play a Force into any position without a Force, subject to any printed deployment restriction.

### Bond and Name

A Bond or Name may be played before its Force. Until a Force arrives it is **prepared**: it stays face-up in that position but contributes no Strength and no Force-dependent rule.

Once a Force is present, apply the Bond and Name normally.

A Bond attached to a Force while that formation has no Name is an **open Bond**.

Every printed Name is **Unique**.

A Name's one-shot **when you play** text resolves only when the Name itself is played, if its requirements can be met then. It does not trigger later merely because a Force or Bond is added.

### Named Formation

A position containing a **Force, Bond, and Name** is a **Named Formation**.

When the missing component completes the formation, resolve any ability that triggers when that formation becomes complete.

Named Formations persist between Battles unless removed or driven off. Incomplete formations do not.

### Deployment restrictions

A restriction such as **Deploy - Frontline only** or **Deploy - Rear only** applies only when that Force enters play.

It does not prevent later movement caused by Retreat or card text.

## Roles and classifications {#roles}

Cards may have roles or classifications such as *Swordsman*, *Spearman*, *Archer*, *Healer*, *Ship*, *Stronghold*, *Skirmisher*, *Human*, *God*, *King*, *Fleet*, or *Warband*.

**Roles and classifications do not carry hidden rules.** Any mechanic associated with a card is printed on that card. Other cards may refer to a role or classification by name.

**Adjacent** means immediately left or right in the same rank. **In front of** and **behind** mean the other rank in the same Front.

## Heroes {#heroes}

Heroes are **Unique** dual-use cards. Play a Hero either as a **Force** or as a **Name**, using the appropriate text on the card.

Each player may play at most **one Hero card from hand per Battle**.

Heroes already present in persistent Named Formations remain on the battlefield and do not use the next Battle's Hero allowance.

## Maneuver {#maneuver}

Maneuver is an operation that costs **1 Command**.

Choose one of your Named Formations and move it:

- exactly one Front left or right;
- in the **same rank**.

If the destination is empty, move there.

If it contains a friendly formation, the two formations **swap positions**. Only the formation initiating the Maneuver must be Named. Move the complete contents of both positions.

Maneuver cannot change rank and cannot occur between Battles. There is no generic vertical movement.

## Stories and Stratagems {#stories}

**Stories** are always played face-up.

- An **immediate Story** resolves and is discarded.
- An **ongoing Story** remains until its own text ends it.
- Each player may have at most **2 ongoing Stories**.

Story forms such as *Legend*, *Saga*, *Myth*, *Omen*, *Warning*, *Prophecy*, and *Conspiracy* have no general rule unless a card refers to them.

A **Stratagem** is a public Battle-wide card. Playing one uses your operation and its printed Command cost. Each player may play at most **one Stratagem from hand per Battle**. It normally remains until the Battle ends.

## Passing {#passing}

Normally you may not Pass until both players have completed at least one operation. If you have no other legal operation, you may Pass.

A Pass means you are willing to end the Battle in its current state. It does **not** end your participation in the Battle.

After a player Passes, the opponent takes a normal turn: draw 1 card, then choose an operation.

- If the opponent also **Passes**, the Battle ends. The Battle therefore ends after **two consecutive Passes**.
- If the opponent takes any other operation, the earlier Pass is cleared and play continues normally.

The player who made the **first of the two consecutive Passes** starts the next Battle.

<div class="rulebook-kicker">ENDING A BATTLE</div>

## Resolve the Fronts {#scoring}

Resolve all four Fronts separately.

For each Front:

1. add the Strength of that player's Frontline and Rear Forces, including role, Bond, Name, Story, Stratagem, and card modifiers;
2. compare the two totals.

Higher Strength wins the Front. Equal Strength is a tie.

A prepared Bond or Name without a Force contributes 0 Strength. Final Force Strength cannot fall below 0.

The margin of victory has no core effect unless a card says otherwise.

## Retreat {#retreat}

After all four Front results are known:

1. **discard all incomplete formations**;
2. on every lost Front, **drive off** any Named Formation in the Rear;
3. then **Retreat** any Named Formation in the Frontline into the Rear.

A driven-off formation is discarded as a complete Force - Bond - Name.

A tied Front does nothing. Formations on a won Front hold their positions.

## After the Battle {#after-battle}

After resolving Retreat:

1. discard Stratagems and end Battle-only effects, including all temporary Strength modifiers marked **this Battle**;
2. leave ongoing Stories in play according to their text;
3. recover Command;
4. check Command Collapse;
5. if the war continues, keep your hand, draw pile, and discard pile, then draw until you have 10 cards;
6. reset the Hero and Stratagem allowances;
7. begin the next Battle with the player who made the first of the two consecutive Passes.

Do not reshuffle merely because a Battle ended. If you must draw from an empty deck, shuffle the discard pile to make a new draw pile.

There is **no between-Battle Maneuver**.

<div class="rulebook-kicker">THE LONG WAR</div>

## Command {#command}

Command is always between **0 and 20**.

Use a d20 to track 20 down to 1. At 0, set the die aside.

After each Battle, recover:

| Battle just ended | Base recovery |
| --- | ---: |
| I | 10 |
| II | 7 |
| III | 5 |
| IV | 4 |
| V | 3 |
| VI | 2 |
| VII | 1 |
| VIII and later | 0 |

Your actual recovery is:

**base recovery - Fronts lost**, minimum 0.

Then add that recovery to your remaining Command, to a maximum of 20.

### Command Collapse

After recovery, if **either player has fewer than 5 Command**, compare Command.

- The player with less Command loses the war.
- If Command is equal, the war continues.

The threshold and recovery schedule are current playtest values.

## Card movement and removal {#reference}

Unless a card says otherwise:

| Event | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Force is discarded | Discard its Bond and Name with it. |
| Bond is discarded | Force stays; return its Name to its owner's hand. |
| Bond is returned | Force stays; put the Bond into its owner's hand. |
| Name is returned or discarded | Force and Bond stay; the Bond becomes open. |
| Force moves | Its Bond and Name move with it. |
| Named Formation Maneuvers or Retreats | Force, Bond, and Name move together. |
| Rear Named Formation is driven off | Discard the complete formation. |

## Timing

There is no reaction stack.

For a card play: announce required targets, pay its cost, resolve it completely, then apply continuing effects.

For Maneuver: announce the formation and destination, pay 1 Command, move or swap, then recalculate positional effects.

Card text that refers to a Front result, Retreat, or Battle end resolves when that event occurs.

## Deck construction

The current playtest format uses:

- exactly **34 cards**;
- exactly **14 Force-type cards**;
- exactly **6 printed Names**;
- maximum 2 copies of a non-Unique title;
- maximum 1 copy of a Unique title.

Heroes count among the Force-type cards and may instead be played as Names.
