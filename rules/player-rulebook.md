# The Long War — D6 paper playtest

*Two-player experimental physical edition. The digital engine uses different rules.*

## Goal

Control Fronts with surviving Forces. Destroyed Forces never return. Lose the war if Command reaches 0 after a Battle (before recovery). If nobody collapses after Battle VIII, more controlled Fronts wins; then more Command; otherwise a draw.

## Set up

Each player needs one **24-card D6 deck**, **20 Command**, four reusable secret **Battle Plans** (Assault, Hold, Volley, Raid), and one D6. Draw 10 cards; optionally reshuffle up to two back and replace them. Choose the starting player at random.

There are four **Fronts**, each with **Frontline, Middle and Rear** positions per player. One Force (or Force-mode Hero) per position. Only Fronts 2 and 3 start open; open Front 1 at Battle II and Front 4 at Battle III. **No Bonds, Names, Tactics, Orders, Stratagems, Narratives or old Opening Orders** in this test.

## On your turn

Before drawing, you may **Pass**. Otherwise draw 1 (hand limit 10), then take up to **two Actions**:

- **Play** a Force into an empty legal position, paying its printed Command cost. Maximum one Hero played per Battle, always as a Force.
- **Maneuver** a Force one orthogonally adjacent legal position for **1 Command**, or swap adjacent friendly Forces if both positions remain legal. No diagonals or inactive Fronts.
- **Cycle** two cards from hand into your ordinary discard pile to draw one.

You may end with unused Actions; that is not Pass. Passing skips the draw and actions. After the first Pass, the opponent gets one complete turn, then the passer gets one complete turn. Neither can Pass again. Then both choose secret Battle Plans.

You cannot voluntarily spend Command below 0.

## Hidden Battle Plans (one per army)

Both players select one reusable Plan secretly and **reveal together before rolling**. Plans never alter Force or the D6.

| Plan | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Assault** | Strike foremost; **one optional forward rank step per Front** after casualties, without requiring an Opportunity |
| **Hold** | Withdraw one Force a rank before combat; Protect may reach any ally in its own Front |
| **Volley** | Archer in **Middle or Rear** may target any occupied enemy rank; other ordinary Strikes target foremost |
| **Raid** | Redeploy one Raider/Rider/Skirmisher from each Front sideways to an originally empty same-rank adjacent active Front; mobile Forces in **Frontline or Middle** can target enemy Middle/Rear, otherwise foremost |

**Strong counters:** **Hold > Assault > Volley > Raid > Hold**. The Plan on the left counters the one on the right. The other two pairs (Assault/Raid and Hold/Volley) are neutral. Mirrors have no counter. A counter is a tactical advantage, **not an automatic victory**.

**Tactical Read:** When your Plan counters theirs, in **each occupied Front**, you may choose **one** rolled Strike or Protect to improve. **Read Strike** (no fixed printed target rank) can choose any enemy rank and ignores one Protect against it. **Read Protect** cancels one additional damaging Strike against an otherwise eligible friend. The Read has no effect if no eligible result/target exists; it never changes dice or Force, and cannot bypass Evade or Rescue. No marker is needed.

**Roles by rank:** A **Frontline Guard** rolling Protect can protect any ally in its own Front (normal capacity). A **Middle/Rear Archer** unlocks Volley targeting. A **Frontline/Middle Raider, Rider or Skirmisher** unlocks Raid's deep targeting. A **Rear Healer/Steward** rolling Rescue may save any ally in its own Front (normal capacity). Printed fixed-target Specials and explicit adjacent-Front Specials retain their text. No passive bonuses.

**Opportunities:** After casualties, if your Strike actually **discarded** an enemy, a Protect/Evade **cancelled a damaging Strike**, or a Rescue **prevented a defeat** in that Front, you may advance **one unpinned surviving Force one empty legal rank forward** in that Front. Assault gets this option without earning an Opportunity. **One post-combat move maximum per player per Front**, including printed Special advances. A Force cannot move twice in the Battle. A full Front has nowhere to advance, and no new status persists.

All pre-combat movements must be declared simultaneously into **originally empty distinct destinations**, observing printed row restrictions. All post-combat movement uses spaces **empty after casualties**. Neither type can cross to the enemy side, enter inactive Fronts, leap occupied ranks or swap.

## Dice and outcomes

Roll **one D6 per player per occupied Front**. Every Force on that player's side of the Front performs its own printed result for the rolled face, even if defeated in the same exchange. Every Force has four differently arranged outcomes. No separate Attack action.

- **Strike:** choose an enemy permitted by your Battle Plan. Players freely choose among eligible targets; printed Special targets override the Plan.
- **Coordinated Strike:** each additional **uncancelled damaging Strike at the same target adds +1 effective Force to every Strike at that target**. If any effective Force is **greater** than the defender's Force, it is defeated. Equal Force is a stalemate unless that Strike's Special says otherwise. **Two Strikes do not automatically kill.**
- **Pressure:** when declaring a Strike that **cannot defeat its target alone**, choose to use it as Pressure instead of damage. After Evade, Pressure suppresses **all Protect/Rescue from the targeted Force** for this Battle; if it rolled neither, Pressure Pins it instead. Pressure gives **no** coordination bonus.
- **Pin:** a Force hit by an uncancelled **failed damaging Strike**, or applicable Pressure, **cannot move after combat this Battle**. It still attacks, supports and holds territory. No persistent Pin marker.
- **Protect:** cancels **one** incoming damaging Strike (or the exact capacity stated on its card). **Evade** cancels all Strikes and Pressure against itself.
- **Rescue:** prevents **one Force's entire defeat**, regardless of how many Strikes would have killed it. It operates **after** lethal attacks, not as an individual hit cancellation.
- **Falter:** nothing. **Special:** use its printed combat operation at the normal universal timing.

A Special explicitly naming an adjacent active Front is the **only** way a Force can affect another Front. “Foremost” is Frontline if occupied, otherwise Middle, otherwise Rear. “Ahead/behind” refers to ranks within the same Front, and a Force is friendly to itself unless stated otherwise. Printed rank restrictions still apply.

## Resolve every Battle in this order

1. **Reveal Plans** and make simultaneous eligible **Hold/Raid** moves.
2. Each player rolls one D6 per occupied Front. Every Force uses its own printed result.
3. **Declare legal Strikes and Pressure simultaneously**; players freely choose eligible targets. If your Plan counters the opponent's, choose at most one **Tactical Read** per Front.
4. Resolve **Evade → Pressure → Protect**. Frontline Guard protection and Hold use their wider rank reach. Read Protect cancels one extra damaging Strike for an eligible friend; Read Strike bypasses one Protect, not Evade or Rescue.
5. **Coordinate:** each uncancelled damaging Strike gains +1 effective Force per other Strike at the same target. Greater Force defeats; equality stalemates unless a Special says otherwise. Unsuccessful uncancelled Strikes **Pin** (no post-combat movement).
6. **Rescue** prevents an entire Force's defeat. Rear Healers/Stewards can save any friendly Force in their Front, while an explicitly printed adjacent-Front Special may rescue elsewhere.
7. Discard unrescued casualties permanently. Check each Front for an **Opportunity** (enemy discarded, damaging Strike cancelled, or successful Rescue). Resolve printed Special advances first, then at most **one post-combat forward move per player per Front** (always permitted by Assault, otherwise only with an Opportunity). No twice-moved or Pinned Forces.
8. Majority of surviving occupied positions **controls the Front**. Ties are contested; rank never scores extra control.
9. Lose 1 Command per enemy-controlled Front, check Collapse at 0, then recover 8 if behind in controlled Fronts, 6 if ahead, 7 each if tied (cap 20); refill hands to 10.

Every Force participates once even if it is defeated in the simultaneous exchange. A Read, Opportunity and Pin never persist to the next Battle. No Force statistic, printed cost, die face or existing card ability is changed by Phase 2.

Recycle only the **ordinary discard pile** when drawing; **casualties never re-enter decks**. If there are no cards to draw, skip the draw. The player who did not Pass starts the next Battle.

For edge cases and a worked example see the [detailed physical rules](rulebook.md). This version is provisional and has not been validated in a tabletop game.
